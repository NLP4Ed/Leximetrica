About Leximetrica

Leximetrica is an open-access quantitative linguistics and lexical dispersion workbench designed to measure dispersion indices, burstiness, and corpus-level statistical laws directly in the browser.

Metrics & Formulas
  • Gries's DP & DPnorm - Deviation of Proportions (0 = uniform, 1 = clumped)
  • Juilland's D & Carroll's D2 - Classic & entropy dispersion (1 = uniform, 0 = clumped)
  • Rosengren's S - Frequency-weighted proportional dispersion index
  • Burstiness (B) - Inter-occurrence gap index (-1 periodic, 0 Poisson, +1 bursty)
  • Zipf & Heaps Laws - Power-law rank decay & vocabulary growth regressions
  • Shannon Entropy & Menzerath-Altmann - Lexical information bits, evenness J, and sentence-word trend
